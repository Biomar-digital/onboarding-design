Safety Comes First

Seen a Safety Incident, Hazard or Near Miss?Follow these steps and report it in Workday.

What should I do if I see an incident?

  1. 1
    Make the situation safe
    • If it is safe to do so, stop the activity.
    • Get help if someone is injured.
    • Secure the area to prevent further harm.
  2. 2
    Inform your supervisor

    As soon as possible.

  3. 3
    Report it in Workday

    Once the situation is under control.

How do I report in Workday?

Scan the QR code and submit a report directly

QR CODE
OR

Report via Workday

  1. Open Workday
  2. Search for “Report Safety Incident”
  3. Complete the form
  4. Submit the report

What information should be included?

  • What happened?
  • Where did it happen?
  • When did it happen?
  • Photos (if available)

Why should I report safety incidents in Workday? Every report helps us:

Prevent future incidents
Identify hazards
Learn from experience
Improve Health & Safety across BioMar
