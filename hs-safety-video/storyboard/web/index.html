Open points before animation

  • Voice-over follows the approved script word for word, with one grammar fix in 4A: “As employee” → “As an employee”.
  • 3A and 3B run in the order the voice-over gives the actions: make the area safe, then seek assistance, then inform the supervisor.
  • All 15 illustrations checked for anatomy (two arms, two hands each), PPE by area (full PPE on the floor, none in the office) and walkway positions.
  • Workday beats 4·1 to 4·4 come from your own screen recording, on phone; personal data will be blurred.
  • Timecodes are estimates at about 150 words per minute and will be locked once the voice-over is recorded.
